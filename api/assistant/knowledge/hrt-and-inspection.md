# Head race tunnels and why this system exists
Source: general background written for this project and the project README

## What a head race tunnel is
A head race tunnel (HRT) carries water from a dam or intake to the turbines of a hydropower plant. It runs full of water under pressure.
Its concrete lining keeps water in and supports the rock around it, so lining damage can lead to leakage and loss of power.

## How inspection is usually done
Traditionally the plant is shut down and the tunnel drained so engineers can walk it, or divers are sent in.
Both cost generation time, and diving in a tunnel is dangerous.

## What this project does
This project's idea is that an underwater ROV records video while the tunnel is in service, and AI finds cracks and spalling in the footage.
Each defect is placed by chainage and clock position on a 3D digital twin, and compared between inspections to see what is new or growing.
Engineers review every finding; the system does not decide repairs.
